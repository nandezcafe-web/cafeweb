Shipping and refund policy

What it costs, when it arrives

And what we do if something goes wrong.

Cost

Shipping costs COP 12,000 anywhere in Colombia and is free on orders from COP 150,000. You see it in the cart before giving us any data.

Time

We ship from Cúcuta with Interrapidísimo. It arrives in 2 to 5 business days; rural areas can take a few days more. We send you the tracking number.

If something goes wrong

If the bag arrives broken, wet or is not what you ordered, write to nandezcafe@gmail.com within 5 days of receiving it, with a photo and your order number. We send it again or refund you, at our cost.

Returns

Coffee is food: once opened we cannot take it back. If you change your mind before we ship, we cancel the order and refund you in full.
