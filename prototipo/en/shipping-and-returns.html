Shipping and refund policy

What it costs, when it arrives

And what we do if something goes wrong.

Cost

Shipping costs COP 12,000 anywhere in Colombia and is free on orders from COP 120,000. You see it in the cart before giving us any data.

Time

We ship from Cúcuta with Interrapidísimo. It arrives in 2 to 5 business days; rural areas can take a few days more. We send you the tracking number.

Pre-order policy

A coffee marked "pre-order" is not on the shelf yet: you pay now and we roast and ship it from 20 Nov 2026.

  • Payment. You pay the full amount when you order, in the cart. The order is confirmed when the payment is approved and you get the receipt by email.
  • Price. The price you see is the final one. Shipping is shown separately in the cart.
  • Limited run. There are 90 bags per coffee in pre-order. When they run out, the coffee shows as sold out. Reservations are served in the order they were paid.
  • Shipping. Everything ships from 20 Nov 2026, in payment order, with Interrapidísimo. You get the tracking number by email. If your order also has a coffee that is available now, the whole order ships together on that date.
  • Delay. If we cannot ship on 20 Nov 2026, we write to you before that date with the new one. You choose: wait for it, or get a full refund.
  • Cancel. You can cancel any time before we ship, for a full refund. Write to nandezcafe@gmail.com with your order number.
  • Refund. We return the money to the same payment method, within 10 business days of the cancellation. Your bank or Mercado Pago may take a few more days to show it.
  • Address. To change the delivery address, write to nandezcafe@gmail.com before the order ships.
  • After it ships. The usual terms apply: see the shipping and refund policy.

If something goes wrong

If the bag arrives broken, wet or is not what you ordered, write to nandezcafe@gmail.com within 5 days of receiving it, with a photo and your order number. We send it again or refund you, at our cost.

Returns

Coffee is food: once opened we cannot take it back. If you change your mind before we ship, we cancel the order and refund you in full.
