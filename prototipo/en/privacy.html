Privacy policy

Your data, only for your order

Policy under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 on personal data.

What we ask for

To ship an order: name, ID or tax number, address, city, department, phone and email. The ID number is what the courier asks for to issue the waybill and what the invoice needs. Nothing else.

The community

If you join the Nandez Coffee Gang community and tick the box, we use your email to send you news and members-only offers. You can leave with the link at the end of every email or by writing to nandezcafe@gmail.com.

What we use it for

To confirm and ship orders and send the notices and news you asked for. We do not sell or share your data, except with the carrier that delivers your order.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to nandezcafe@gmail.com. We answer within 15 business days.

Who is responsible
