02   How we work

Not “Colombian coffee”. Somebody's coffee.

  1. You know where it comes from.Every bag names its farm. The blend names its two: Castillo from Ragonvalia and El Paraíso from Chinácota, nothing else.
  2. Data with a signature.Altitude, variety, process and score say who checked them and when. What we have not verified yet, we do not make up.
  3. Three coffees, not thirty.A lot goes on the shelf only if we cup it and it convinces us. We would rather sell out than fill the shelf.
  4. Whole bean or ground.Two coffees go whole bean, so you grind them right before brewing. The blend goes medium ground, ready for the cone or the drip machine. All three, medium roast.
